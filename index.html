What I do

I make complex systems
observable, testable, reliable.

My work sits where safety-critical software meets practical engineering: understanding code deeply, proving its behavior, and removing friction from the workflow around it.

At KPIT Technologies, I work on Cummins Base Software projects across communication, memory, I/O, and Complex Device Driver domains. My focus spans white-box testing, VectorCAST verification, requirements analysis, ASPICE, and Functional Safety activities.

Beyond verification, I develop embedded firmware in C, implement protocols including CAN and Modbus, create device drivers, and build Python automation for engineering teams.
