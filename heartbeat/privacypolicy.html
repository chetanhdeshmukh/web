Privacy Policy for Heartbeat

Last Updated: December 24, 2025

Chetan Deshmukh ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Heartbeat mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information from our users.

1. Personal and Sensitive Information We Collect

To provide our core service of managing relationship frequency and "heartbeats," our app requires access to the following sensitive data:

  • Call Logs: We access your device's call history (READ_CALL_LOG) to calculate the time since your last interaction with a specific contact. This data is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Contacts: We access your contact list (READ_CONTACTS) to allow you to select specific "loved ones" to track. This information is processed locally and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Device Identifiers: We collect and share your Advertising ID (GAID) with our advertising partners to provide relevant advertisements.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To display the "Heartbeat" status (time since last call) for your selected contacts.
  • Advertising: To show personalized or contextual ads through our third-party ad networks.
  • App Updates: To ensure you are using the latest version of the app for security and feature stability via Google Play services.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. However, we share certain non-sensitive data (Advertising ID) with the following partners for advertising and analytics:

  • ironSource (LevelPlay): For ad mediation and serving. View Privacy Policy
  • InMobi: For ad delivery. View Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: For app performance and mandatory update checks.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: All call log and contact data accessed by the app remains on your device. We do not store this data on external servers.
  • Account Deletion: Since we do not maintain a centralized user database for your call history, uninstalling the app or clearing the app cache will remove all locally stored preferences and cached data.
  • Advertising Data: Our ad partners have their own retention policies (typically 13 months for de-identified data).

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Heartbeat is a general audience app and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take immediate steps to delete it from our logs.

6. User Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to access, update, or delete your data. You can manage your ad preferences via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
